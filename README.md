#### Results
##### Indonesia government debt has continued to increase significantly each year. Over the five-year period from 2021 to 2025, the total government debt rose from IDR 6,914 trillion to IDR 9,658.2 trillion, representing an increase of approximately **39.7 percent**. Furthermore, as of the first semester of 2026, government debt had increased further to **IDR 10,293.7 trillion**.

This significant increase in government debt stands in contrast to state revenue, which has remained relatively low and fluctuated over the years. In 2025, government debt was equivalent to **3.49 times the amount of state revenue**.
